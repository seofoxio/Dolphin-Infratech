Search Engine Optimization

SEO strategy: dominate local Google search & Maps

When people search for waterproofing, they almost always need a local specialist. Our 7-step SEO framework focuses heavily on high-intent terms like "waterproofing + city", Google Business Profile rankings in Google Maps, and high-converting service landing pages.

We identify the exact search queries entered by desperate property owners—such as "terrace waterproofing near me", "bathroom leak repair without breaking tiles", "water tank leakage treatment", and "PU injection grouting contractors". Each keyword cluster is mapped to a dedicated service or city landing page.

Target: Bottom-of-funnel customer searches

We optimize title tags, meta descriptions, H1/H2 header hierarchies, internal cross-linking, image alt tags, and FAQ schema markup across every service page and geographic city page to maximize Google crawl efficiency.

Outcome: Higher click-through rates (CTR) on Google

We optimize your Google Business Profile with complete categories, service lists, high-res photos, weekly geo-tagged updates, and an automated SMS/WhatsApp workflow to solicit 5-star Google reviews from satisfied clients.

Goal: Top 3 Local Pack ranking in Google Maps

Two comprehensive, SEO-optimized articles published each month answering common homeowner questions: waterproofing cost per sq. ft., acrylic vs. polyurethane coatings, pre-monsoon society terrace maintenance, and basement moisture prevention.

Impact: Long-tail Google traffic + establishes industry authority

Consistent Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) syndication across trusted Indian business directories (Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, TradeIndia) and niche construction/architecture industry portals to build domain authority.

Deliverable: 10–15 verified local listings monthly

Continuous monitoring of Google Core Web Vitals, mobile viewport usability, XML sitemap indexing, robots.txt validity, 404 broken link redirection, and SSL security certification.

Benefit: Zero indexing penalties, 100% Google compliance

Detailed monthly executive reports summarizing keyword rank movements, total organic website visits, phone call clicks, WhatsApp taps, contact form inquiries, and Google Maps views.
